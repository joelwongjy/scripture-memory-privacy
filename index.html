Scripture Memory — Privacy Policy

Effective: June 23, 2026

Scripture Memory is designed to protect your privacy. We do not collect, store, or share any personal data.

Data collection

The app collects no personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking. The app works fully offline and makes no network requests to us.

Your data stays with you

Your memorization progress, settings, and reminders are stored on your device. If you enable iCloud, this data syncs across your devices through your personal Apple iCloud account. We have no access to it. iCloud data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, notifications are scheduled locally on your device. No notification data is sent to us or anyone else.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes its on-device data. iCloud-synced data can be managed or removed from Settings > [your name] > iCloud on your device.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

We may update this policy; changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
